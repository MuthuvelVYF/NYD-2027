Spiritual Inspiration & Heritage

Arise with purpose. Run with conviction.

Swami Vivekananda (1863–1902) inspired modern India with his clarion message of self-confidence, physical vigor, and selfless service. In February 1897, his historic return to Chennai was celebrated at Castle Kernan (now Vivekanandar Illam on Kamarajar Salai, Marina Beach), where his inspiring words awakened the nation's youth.

Celebrated nationwide on 12 January as National Youth Day, his birth anniversary reminds us that a healthy body and a resolute mind are the true pillars of service. The Vivekananda Youth Forum (youth wing of Sri Ramakrishna Math, Chennai) organizes this annual marathon to put that vision into motion—uniting students, professionals, and citizens in a celebration of endurance and noble character.

“Arise, awake, and stop not till the goal is reached.”

Swami Vivekananda
01

Muscles of Iron, Nerves of Steel

Physical stamina and moral courage as the indispensable foundation for personal and national growth.

02

Atmano Mokshartham Jagad Hitaya Cha

The sacred motto of the Ramakrishna Order: for one’s own spiritual growth and the welfare of the world.

03

Be and Make

Swami Vivekananda’s motto for youth: first build yourself into a person of character, then inspire others.
